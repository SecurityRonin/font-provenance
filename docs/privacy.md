# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-07-24*

## Summary

font-provenance is a research publication — a reference paper and a citation register, distributed as static documents. It does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

## Data Access

The repository contains only documents (LaTeX/PDF/markdown). Nothing in it executes on your data, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Telemetry

font-provenance has **no telemetry**. The documents make no network requests of any kind. (Following citation hyperlinks takes you to the cited third-party sites, which have their own privacy policies.)

## Open Source

font-provenance is open source (Apache-2.0). You can audit every source and citation at [github.com/SecurityRonin/font-provenance](https://github.com/SecurityRonin/font-provenance).
